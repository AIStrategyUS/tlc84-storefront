# Photo shot list

The mockup uses the client's real photos where the reference material had
them (Custom Photo Magnets, Custom Photo Keychains, Save the Date Magnets,
Logo/QR Magnets, Baby Blanket), and a stylized colored-circle placeholder
everywhere else. These are the shots that would replace those placeholders
and round out the site for a real launch. All of them should be clean,
well-lit product shots on a simple, uncluttered background (a plain surface
or light backdrop), shot straight-on so the client can drop each one in
without changing any layout.

1. **Custom Photo Pins** — 3 to 4 finished pins, straight-on, at least one
   showing the pin-back clasp so shoppers understand what they're buying.

2. **Birth Month Flower Magnet** — one clean shot of a single design (any
   month), plus an optional second shot showing 3 to 4 different months laid
   out together to show the variety.

3. **Bible Verse Magnet** — 2 to 3 of the verse designs laid out together
   (e.g. "Be Still," "Grace," "Fearfully & Wonderfully Made") so the range of
   styles is visible at a glance.

4. **Sport Fan Magnet or Pin** — one shot per format (magnet and pin) of a
   generic/neutral sport design, since this product covers 8 sports and 5
   roles; a couple of concrete examples (e.g. soccer, football) will read as
   representative without needing all 40 combinations photographed.

5. **Patriotic Magnet** — one straight-on shot of the finished design.

6. **Christmas Coloring Magnet** — one shot of the finished (colored-in)
   magnet, and ideally one of the blank/colorable version if that's a
   distinct product.

7. **Punny Magnet Gift Set** — the full set of 6 laid out together, since
   the product is sold as a fixed set, not a single item.

8. **ABC 123 Magnet Kit** — the full kit laid out (letters + numbers), plus
   a close-up of the optional custom name plaque if one is available to
   photograph.

9. **Family photo for the About page** — a real photo of the family behind
   the business, to replace the placeholder on `/about`. Doesn't need to be
   a product shot; a natural, warm photo is enough.

10. **A lifestyle/hero shot (optional)** — a magnet actually on a fridge, or
    a keychain on an actual keyring, for future use in marketing or a
    future homepage refresh. Not required to replace anything currently on
    the site, since the current homepage hero is an illustrated graphic by
    design (the brief asked to avoid a stock-photo hero), but useful to have
    on hand.

## Framing notes for all shots

- Shoot on a plain, light-colored surface or backdrop (the current site's
  patterned-wallpaper background reads as a phone snapshot, not a product
  photo).
- Fill the frame with the product; avoid the wide margins seen in some of
  the existing photos.
- Natural or soft diffused light, no harsh shadows or reflections on the
  glossy magnet surface.
- Shoot square or close to it (1:1), since every product card and the
  customizer frame both crop to a circle from a square source.
