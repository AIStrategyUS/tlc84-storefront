# Photo shot list

Every product in the catalog now has at least one real photo from the
client's own site (`static.wixstatic.com`) — none are placeholders anymore.
That said, the photo quality across the catalog is inconsistent: some are
clean, well-lit, professionally shot (Birth Month Flower Magnets, Custom
Photo Pins, ABC 123 Magnet Kit, Punny Magnet Gift Set), while others are
phone snapshots on a patterned tablecloth or a plain gray design proof
rather than a photographed product. The list below prioritizes what would
most improve the site if the client can get new photography, roughly in
order of impact.

1. **Custom Photo Keychains** — the current photo shows text/quote
   keychains, not photo keychains, since that's the only keychain photo
   that exists in the client's material. A straight-on shot of an actual
   photo keychain (the product this listing sells) would fix a real
   mismatch between the photo and the product.

2. **Baby Blanket** — the current photo is cropped out of a Wix marketing
   graphic (a torn-paper template with a title baked in); a clean shot of
   the actual blankets, in the range of color combinations offered, would
   read much better than a crop of a graphic.

3. **Custom Photo Magnets, Save the Date Magnets, Logo/QR Magnets** — the
   existing photos are real and usable but are phone snapshots on a busy
   patterned background. A reshoot on a plain, light surface, filling the
   frame with the product, would bring these up to the same quality as the
   catalog's better photos.

4. **Bible Verse Magnets** — the current primary photo is a real, clean
   product shot; a second design-proof-style image is also in use as the
   hover photo. Real photos of 2 to 3 more verse designs, shot the same way
   as the primary, would round this out further, since the product covers
   15 verse designs.

5. **Sport Fan Magnets and Pins** — the two photos in use are real but show
   only two of the many sport/role combinations. A couple more (e.g. a
   soccer or volleyball design) would better represent the range.

6. **Family photo for the About page** — no photo of the family behind the
   business exists anywhere in the reference material, so `/about` uses a
   plain placeholder rather than a product photo standing in for a person.
   Doesn't need to be a product shot; a natural, warm photo is enough.

7. **A lifestyle/hero shot (optional)** — a magnet actually on a fridge, or
   a keychain on an actual keyring, for future use in marketing or a future
   homepage refresh. Not required to replace anything currently on the
   site, since the homepage hero is an illustrated graphic by design (the
   brief asked to avoid a stock-photo hero), but useful to have on hand.

## Framing notes for any reshoot

- Shoot on a plain, light-colored surface or backdrop. Several existing
  photos use a busy patterned wallpaper background that reads as a phone
  snapshot rather than a product photo.
- Fill the frame with the product; avoid the wide margins seen in some of
  the existing photos.
- Natural or soft diffused light, no harsh shadows or reflections on the
  glossy magnet surface.
- Shoot square or close to it (1:1), since every product card and the
  customizer frame both crop to a circle from a square source.
